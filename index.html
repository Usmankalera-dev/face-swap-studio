What affects quality?

  • Front-facing faces with similar angles swap best. Side profiles and covered faces will look off — that is a limit of landmark-based swapping, not a bug.
  • Matching light and skin tone helps. The photo mode adjusts the source face colour toward the target before blending.
  • The swap replaces the face region only. Hair, ears and background stay from the target.
  • Video is processed live in your browser, so longer videos take longer and need a capable device.
